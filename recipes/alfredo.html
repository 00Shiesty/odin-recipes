for this recipe you dont need a whole lot, for the ready made or from scratch. the ready made is super simple you just need a can of alfredo and boxed pasta. boil then combine lol. The from scrath method is my fav way of making it. its only 4 ingredients lol

Ingredients

  • flour
  • egg
  • butter
  • parmesan
  • salt
  1. start by mixing the flour and egg until a dough ball is formed
  2. next kneed until it pulls back when you stretch it
  3. roll it thin wall until you can press a finger against it and it shows
  4. cut into thin lines then put into boiling water should take about 3-4 minutes salt the water
  5. on a plate but a bout 2 tablespoons of butter then top with hot pasta and mix in parmesan combine until its ready
